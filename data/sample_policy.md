# DEMO CONTENT - sample store policy (edit or replace with your own)

## Late payment fee
If an invoice stays unpaid for more than 15 days after its due date, a one-time late fee of Rs 100 is added. The fee is added only once per invoice. If you tell us before the due date that you need more time, we can note a new date and the fee is not added during that period.
Also asked as: late fee kya hai; der se payment karne par extra charge; extra paisa lagega kya; penalty lagti hai; delay charge; kitna extra dena padega

## Refund policy
Refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them. Refund requests must be raised within 30 days of the payment. Amounts paid by UPI return to the same UPI account.
Also asked as: refund kaise milega; paisa wapas kab aayega; paisa wapas kitne din mein; money back; return policy; wapas milega kya

## Accepted payment methods
You can pay using UPI, debit cards, credit cards and net banking. Payment is always made through a secure payment link that we send you. We never ask for card numbers, PINs or one-time passwords over a call.
Also asked as: kaun kaun se payment option hain; UPI se pay kar sakta hoon; card se payment; net banking chalega; kaise pay karun; payment kaise hoga

## Updating your bank account for auto-debit
To change the bank account used for automatic monthly payments, open the re-mandate link we send you and approve a new mandate for the new account. The old mandate stops once the new one is active. Before any retry of a failed debit you receive a notice at least 24 hours earlier.
Also asked as: mandate kaise update karun; bank account change karna hai; naya account lagana hai; autopay band kaise karun; auto debit ka account badalna; retry se pehle notice milega kya

## Raising a dispute
If you believe an invoice or a charge is wrong, tell us within 7 days. A member of our team reviews the dispute within 2 working days and, while it is under review, we stop automated reminders for that invoice.
Also asked as: bill galat hai kya karun; dispute kaise karun; charge galat laga; complaint kaise karun; invoice mein galti hai

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 6 PM Indian time. Calls from this assistant happen only between 8 AM and 7 PM.
Also asked as: support kab tak khula rehta hai; aapka timing kya hai; kis time baat ho sakti hai; office hours; kab call kar sakta hoon

## Plans
The Basic plan costs Rs 499 per month and the Pro plan costs Rs 999 per month. Both are billed monthly and can be cancelled before the next billing date.
